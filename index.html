About Elyssa

A love of words.
A life shaped by stories.

Elyssa Latham has always found comfort in books and the power of written words. What began with childhood reading and school journals gradually grew into a passion for poetry and storytelling.

Encouraged by a teacher who recognized her talent, Elyssa began to see writing as more than a hobby. It became a way to explore her experiences, express difficult emotions, and connect with others through the written word.

Today, she writes to give a voice to feelings that are often left unspoken, reminding readers that vulnerability is human and that meaningful art can grow from even the hardest experiences.
